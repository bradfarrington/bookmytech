# Task 73: jobs whose charged parts change

Generated 2026-11-17 from the full HaynesPro repair lists of a 2024 Ford Ranger (S28BSW) and a 2010 Ford Fiesta (FN60KYP). A HaynesPro job is the same job on every car, so this is the rule's effect everywhere those jobs appear. Review before release.

- Job types that name a part type: **1196**
- Jobs that stop charging a part: **178** (169 become labour only; 9 keep their fluids, gaskets or bolts)
- Jobs that list one part type twice, or a part and its kit, and now charge one: **34**
- Every job starting Renew, Replace, Fit, Retrofit, Install, Repair, Overhaul or Recondition is unchanged.
