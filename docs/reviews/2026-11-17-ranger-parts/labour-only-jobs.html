Jobs that stop charging for parts

S28 BSW2024 Ford Ranger 3.0 TDCi

Every Ranger repair whose parts charge changes under the new rule. Each one shows every part type HaynesPro lists for the job. Green parts are still charged; struck-through red ones are no longer priced.

The rule
  • Jobs starting Renew, Replace, Fit, Retrofit, Install, Repair, Overhaul or Recondition charge everything HaynesPro lists, as before. They're not on this page unless a part was listed twice.
  • Every other job (check, charge, clean, adjust, bleed, remove/refit, drain/refill, top up, "difficult removal" add-ons) charges only fluids at a full fill, and the gaskets, seals and bolt sets a refit needs.
  • Where a job lists a part and its kit, or the same part twice, only one is charged: the kit (or the second) when NAPA prices it.
