FAQ

Questions, answered.

How are mechanics vetted?
Every mechanic on Book My Tech is DBS-checked, fully insured, and holds a recognised trade qualification. We verify documents directly with the issuing bodies before they take their first job.
Do I pay upfront?
No. We pre-authorise the payment when you book, but only charge when the job's finished and you've confirmed it's been done properly. If we don't fix it, you don't pay.
Is the price I see the price I pay?
Yes. Our quotes include parts, labour and call-out — no hidden fees. If the job turns out to be different from what we quoted, we'll talk you through it before any work begins.
What if the mechanic can't fix the problem?
A diagnostic inspection is a set price (from £59.99). Your mechanic finds the fault, tells you what it needs and quotes for the repair — you decide whether to go ahead.
What if I'm not happy with the work?
Every job's backed by a 12-month parts and labour guarantee. If something isn't right, message us in-app and we'll send a mechanic back at no extra charge.
What areas do you cover?
We're live across Greater London, Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, Leeds and Glasgow, with more cities coming through 2026. Drop your postcode in the hero above to check who covers your street.
